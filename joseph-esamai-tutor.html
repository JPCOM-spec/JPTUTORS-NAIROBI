About

I tutor Integrated Science for Grade 7–9, and Chemistry and Biology at IGCSE and A-Level (Year 10–13), across both the Pearson Edexcel and Cambridge (CAIE) syllabuses. My students are usually looking for a second pair of eyes — someone to unpick a topic slowly, build the exam technique around it, and check it actually stuck. My own background is in biochemistry, so I tend to teach from mechanism outward: understand why a cell divides the way it does, and the exam question stops being a memory test.
